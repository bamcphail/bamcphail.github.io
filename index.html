Theses

  • Can You Cook? Great PMs deliver outcomes, not a skills checklist.

How I work

  • Judge the cook by what leaves the kitchen — outcomes, not a résumé of tools.
  • Treat signal as the scarce resource. Process and headcount do not fix a bad read of the market.
  • Move between product, go-to-market, and building without waiting for permission.
  • Run an operating system: ingest noise, structure it, tie it to outcomes, act the same day.
